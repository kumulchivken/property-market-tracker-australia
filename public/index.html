For the latest monthly release, visit SQM Research media releases. SQM’s terms restrict copying, public display, automated extraction and unauthorised framing of its content.

Area-wide market clocks

Choose a geography for regional market indicators. These clocks are independent of saved properties and portfolio filters. Official regional feeds refresh automatically each day at about 2:00am Brisbane time.

How to read this: The hand shows the direction of the latest published area-wide property indicator, not a measured or official property-cycle phase. Australia and Queensland use ABS mean dwelling prices; Queensland local-government areas use statutory land-valuation changes. These releases are periodic, not live. Locality cards use the selected region as an explicitly labelled proxy where no verified locality series is available. Other geographies show N/A until a supported public series is available.
